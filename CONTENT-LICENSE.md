## Why share-alike

CC BY-SA keeps the question bank an open commons: anyone may use, adapt, and redistribute the content, provided they give attribution and license their derivatives under the same terms. This protects FreeCAT's content from being enclosed in a closed product.

## Hard rule: original or openly-licensed only

Every contributed question, passage, and lesson MUST be either:

1. **Original** — written for FreeCAT (e.g., AI-drafted and then human-reviewed), or
2. **Openly licensed** — imported from a source whose license is compatible with CC BY-SA 4.0, with attribution recorded.

**Never** copy, paraphrase, or adapt questions from AAMC, UWorld, Kaplan, Princeton Review, Blueprint, or any other copyrighted exam-prep material. Doing so is a copyright violation and will be rejected in review.

Users importing their own Anki decks for personal study is a separate matter, handled entirely on the user's own machine — FreeCAT itself distributes no third-party decks.
