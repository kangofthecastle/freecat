Enzymes

Enzymes are protein catalysts that lower the activation energy of a reaction without being consumed. They do not change the equilibrium — only the rate at which it is reached.

Key ideas

  • The active site binds substrate (induced-fit model).
  • Activity depends on temperature and pH.
  • Inhibitors: competitive (raise apparent Km) vs. noncompetitive (lower Vmax).

Check: Does an enzyme change the equilibrium constant of a reaction?

No — enzymes speed up both forward and reverse rates equally, so Keq is unchanged.
