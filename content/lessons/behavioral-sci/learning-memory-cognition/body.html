Learning, Memory & Cognition

Classical conditioning pairs a neutral stimulus with a reflex; operant conditioning shapes behavior through reinforcement and punishment.

Memory stores

Sensory → short-term/working → long-term. Encoding strategies (e.g., elaborative rehearsal) move information into durable storage.

Check: A rat presses a lever more often after receiving food. Which type of conditioning is this?

Operant conditioning — behavior increases because it is followed by positive reinforcement.
